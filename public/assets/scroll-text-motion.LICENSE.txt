Grade collections-scroll.js adapts the native-scroll/progress-driven typography
technique from Codrops ScrollTextMotion:
https://github.com/codrops/ScrollTextMotion
https://github.com/codrops/ScrollTextMotion/blob/main/js/index.js

The original uses Flip on whole text elements. Grade uses its installed GSAP
3.15 SplitText line masks and existing collection covers instead. ScrollSmoother,
ScrambleText, demo media, global styles and demo GSAP files are not included.
GSAP retains its separate license in /assets/gsap.LICENSE.txt.
